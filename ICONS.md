# bwVerify icons

This document identifies the source and licensing of the icon assets bundled with bwVerify.

## Application icons

The bwVerify application and menu icons are licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later.

Source: <https://github.com/bulkware/bwverify>

Applies to:

- `src/bwverify/icons/menu-dark.svg`
- `src/bwverify/icons/menu-light.svg`
- `src/bwverify/icons/org.bulkware.bwverify-symbolic.svg`
- `src/bwverify/icons/org.bulkware.bwverify.svg`
- `data/icons/hicolor/scalable/apps/org.bulkware.bwverify.svg`
- `data/icons/hicolor/symbolic/apps/org.bulkware.bwverify-symbolic.svg`

## Oxygen icons

The Oxygen icons come from the KDE Oxygen icon theme and are licensed under LGPL-3.0-or-later.

Source: <https://develop.kde.org/frameworks/oxygen-icons/>

Applies to: `src/bwverify/icons/oxygen/*.svg`.

## Tango icons

The Tango Desktop Project and Unofficial Tango Icons are in the public domain.

Sources: <https://github.com/sebkur/tango-icon-theme> and
<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Tango_icons>

Applies to: `src/bwverify/icons/tango/*.svg`.
